01 · The pain point

The problem isn't just finding a charger.

EV drivers can face a chain of uncertainty: the charger may be offline, occupied, incompatible, payment may fail, or the displayed status may be stale.

01 / ARRIVE

You reach the station.

The app said it was available. Reality may be different.

02 / DISCOVER

Something doesn't work.

Broken connector, payment issue, network failure, queue, or inaccurate status.

03 / RECOVER

You need a backup.

You search again, spend time and battery, and hope the next charger is reliable.

chargemyEV is exploring one simple question: “What is the probability that this charger will successfully charge me?”
